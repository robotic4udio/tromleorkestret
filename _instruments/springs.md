**The Springs** is a percussion instrument built from salvaged springs from cars and other industry. The springs are mounted directly on the machine.

### **How it works**

1. **Springs as triggers**
    - Hitting a spring triggers a sample or a synthesized tone. The synthetic sound can be modulated by the spring’s own resonance.
    - The sounds can be swapped during a performance.
2. **Spring resonance**
    - A pair of **contact microphones** pick up the vibrations of all the springs. That signal runs through a DSP effects chain, and can also modulate the synthetic sound.
3. **Lamps**
    - Each spring has a **glow lamp** that blinks when the spring is struck.
4. **Two audio systems**
    - **Bela**: an embedded **Bela.io** platform running custom C++ code.
    - **Max for Live**: a **Max4Live device** in Ableton Live, which maps the springs differently and gives other sounds.
        ![AutoXylo]({{site.baseurl}}/images/springs/M4L-SpringChordMapper.png)
    - The two systems work independently.

### **On stage**

- Some of the springs trigger a burst from the flamethrower mounted in the machine.
- The springs can also change the lights on the machine.
