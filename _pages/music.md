We play psychedelic barrel-organ music. It draws on krautrock, psychedelic jazz and experimental electronic music, and it is played on the machine: a [robotic slide bass]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/robotic-slide-bass/), an [automated xylophone]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/autoxylo/) and [mechanical drums]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/mechanic-drums/), together with the instruments we play by hand.

## The tracks {#tracks}

### MoonWolf
A polyrhythmic piece with acoustic and electronic parts. Recorded live at Karrusel Festival 2025.

### Aeolian Horizon
A piece led by [the Aeolynth]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/aeolynth/), our homebuilt wind instrument, inspired by Hans Zimmer and Ludwig Göransson. It starts with breath-like tones and deep drones and builds up in rhythmic layers.

### The Introducer
Written as an introduction to the machine. Electronic dance music with the [robotic bass]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/robotic-slide-bass/), the [keyboard]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/automated-keyboard/), the [drums]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/mechanic-drums/) and glitchy noises from the [Sound Gloves]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/sound-gloves/).

### The Midnight Carousel
From 2024. Techno meets circus music from hell: the machine plays a manic, trance-like rhythm with skewed harmonies, and on top I play [the Springs]({{site.baseurl}}/the-machine/springs/), which add metallic percussion and dissonance. The carousel spins faster and faster until the rhythm falls apart.

## The aftershow {#aftershow}
When there is room for it, we play an aftershow where we open up the machine. The audience can come close, ask questions and see how the instruments work.

### Movie medley

Film themes played by the machine: Hedwig’s Theme and the Quidditch Match from _Harry Potter_, _Indiana Jones_, _Pirates of the Caribbean_, the Imperial March from _Star Wars_, _The Pink Panther_, _Shaun the Sheep_ and Friend Like Me from _Aladdin_.
