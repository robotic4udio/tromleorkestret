The Machine

A steampunk orchestra on wheels

Every instrument is handmade and full of surprises. There are robotic basslines that glide like clockwork, a keyboard that plays itself, drums hit by machines, and a wind instrument that plays chords. They are wired together with custom electronics and code so they respond to motion, touch and sound in real time.
